# Seenry Variants

Resolve the brief, facts, brand constraints, real content and host footprint first. Choose one consequential question: reading order, evidence, density, type relationship, copy argument, interaction mechanism or color distribution. Keep facts and required behavior constant across candidates. Varying fonts, colors and corners together produces untraceable preferences.

Build two or three candidates that answer the question differently. For a page, compare the decisive opening and its handoff. For a component, use the actual product host. Use real or clearly labeled provisional material. A second candidate needs a structural or behavioral difference, not a tinted copy. When a reference informs a candidate, record the observed relationship and original adaptation, not copied pixels or assets.

Render all candidates at equal desktop and narrow sizes and exercise the decisive state. Each must clear basic action, keyboard, focus and content requirements before aesthetic comparison. Present the variants without the author's preferred label where possible. Compare subject fit, hierarchy, material quality, task cost and implementation feasibility. Keep a record of rejected candidates and why.

Do not manufacture a winner. If all candidates are weak, return to the brief or source material. If one wins, carry its relationships into a full slice and review again; a promising hero alone does not validate a website. For a full Seenry installation, [interface exploration](../seenry/references/interface-exploration.md) gives a deeper route, and [Seenry Review](../seenry-review/SKILL.md) can assess the chosen output.
