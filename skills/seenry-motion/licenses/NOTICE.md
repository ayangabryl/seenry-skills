# Motion guidance attribution

`SKILL.md`, the [recipes](../references/recipes.md), [recipes-state](../references/recipes-state.md), [gate](../references/gate.md), [choreography](../references/choreography.md), [performance](../references/performance.md), [vocabulary](../references/vocabulary.md) and [fluid](../references/fluid.md) guides adapt MIT-licensed material by Emil Kowalski: his `animate`, `find-animation-opportunities`, `remove-animations`, `choreograph`, `animation-performance`, `animation-vocabulary`, `gestures`, `emil-design-eng` and `apple-design` skills, first at revisions `85e8e2363b713506e1d5b6e07a0eb2da66be1bc3` and `715c1807e08c497cfd1dc4feb450a2a3300ef2a9`, reviewed again at `64eb3deb9a5fbfa041faf783e0bc3fd635872e9c`. What is adapted: the frequency gate, the ban on `ease-in` and on entrances from `scale(0)`, origin-aware popovers, casting and relating a group, track and release phases for gestures, hold to confirm, and the blurred seam. It is rewritten in Seenry's own words. The original copyright and permission notice is retained in [emil-LICENSE](emil-LICENSE). No endorsement is implied.

The durations, curves and layer relationships are Seenry's own, measured from recordings of shipped products in [production motion](../references/production-motion.md).

transitions.dev by Jakub Antalik was studied for coverage (which component transitions a motion skill should have a recipe for) and for the idea of a motion score. Its licence allows use but not redistribution as a competing library, so no code, selector, token name or prose from it is included here.
