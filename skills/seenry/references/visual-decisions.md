# Decide the visual system before expanding it

“Minimal,” “editorial” and “premium” are insufficient specifications. Define the intended impression through content, relationships and behavior, then test the choices in small working alternatives.

## Layout, type and color

Start from task and reading order. Establish the outer frame, safe area, columns, alignment anchors and groups. An 8px base grid and three principal text roles are useful starting constraints; optical correction and dense data can require other intervals. Equal numbers do not ensure equal perceived spacing. Inspect baselines, icons, line endings and varying text lengths at real size.

Render actual headings, labels, paragraphs, numbers and punctuation in a short font shortlist at comparable perceived sizes. Check supported weights, scripts, font loading and fallback metrics. One family can suffice. Inter, Nunito and Open Runde are choices of character and coverage, not quality tiers. Verify the files: do not assume Open Runde has every Inter feature, weight or an italic. Serif, italic and uppercase need a content role; labels should remain easy to scan. Do not use a decorative contrast pair merely to signal sophistication.

Separate foreground, canvas, panel, action, emphasis and semantic-state color roles. Estimate their occupied area. Put two or three palette options on the **same composition**. Judge focal competition, contrast, imagery and audience fit. A large tinted surface has more influence than a small swatch. For image-led minimal work, compare a neutral surround with any proposed image-derived tint. Brand color may belong only in artwork or action. Rich color and monochrome are both available; neither guarantees polish. Test supported appearances rather than adding a dark toggle by habit.

## Enclosure and control emphasis

Try proximity and alignment before adding a box. A boundary should clarify grouping, editing, selection or elevation. Avoid nesting outlines that repeat the same separation. A shadow can explain a floating layer; a glow needs a subject or state role. Remove decoration in a controlled comparison before declaring a global ban.

Corner geometry belongs to the object. For a uniformly inset rounded child, `inner radius ≈ max(0, outer radius - inset)` is a geometric starting point. It does not prescribe an independent button's radius. Check unequal insets, border thickness and optical fit. Do not make every layer a capsule.

A filled container can establish a primary commit action. For a familiar secondary media control, a filled **icon** with a quiet background may be more legible than a boxed outline symbol. For an unfamiliar action, retain its label. Choose an established icon library and verify optical weight, variant and hit area. An unboxed icon still needs a reachable target, accessible name, focus and discoverable feedback. Morph genuine state changes; do not animate every icon for effect.

Keep the button label and icon grouped. Begin with content width, sensible inline padding and a compact gap; use a full-width action when the surrounding form/task benefits. `space-between` in an oversized button can detach its arrow. Preserve position through loading/success with an anchored icon slot and a considered label strategy, not arbitrary excess width. Technical abbreviations such as RGB can retain uppercase; an unrelated all-caps badge adds no information.

Inspect type, color area and contour relationships together at desktop and narrow width before extending the system. At the final pass compare the page sequence and task states with the reference's visible strengths. A clean hero cannot compensate for a cramped modal or unconsidered footer. Keep rejected treatments and the observation that changed the choice.
