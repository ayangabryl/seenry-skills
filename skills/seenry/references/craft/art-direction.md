# Art direction: give the subject an organizing idea

Use for a new website or a rejected concept that remains generic despite clean execution. A narrow UI fix does not require a new identity.

## Execute

State audience, offer, proof and next action using supplied facts. Mark proposed content as fictional. Map visitor questions to evidence before naming an aesthetic. A useful section answers a question; it does not exist because a common landing-page template contains it.

For a product that helps people make something, identify the finished work the visitor needs to judge. Process diagrams and claims about craft do not replace it. Website design may need a resolved page composition, imagery and reading sequence; a small working widget alone does not establish that range. A widget can be suitable evidence for a component tool. Do not force a fictional app into every creative-tool website. Validate the dominant material at its intended size; a technically complete but weak illustration remains weak evidence. Label authored studies honestly, without presenting them as measured benchmarks or customer work. Visible controls still need meaningful outcomes. An editable task map can itself be a workflow editor's relevant product evidence.

Develop three organizing ideas with identical facts. Name each structural difference: for a restoration studio, comparing a repair, inspecting material or following a project sequence. A palette swap is not a distinct concept. Keep the task operable in every decisive slice. Judge these at realistic scale with a neutral picker outside the product UI.

Resolve one promising slice using actual copy and feasible assets before expanding. If the idea depends on photography or video, find and inspect suitable permitted material first. A geometric placeholder proves layout only, never imagery quality. Record crop, fallback, provenance and the weak point if an asset fails.

Carry two or three relationships through opening, evidence, a small control and closure. Consider visual density and pace across the whole page. Motion should express a subject-relevant operation or support understanding; a shader is not an argument by itself.

## Counterexample

A dramatic process reveal can delay an urgent service inquiry. A quiet project index may serve returning clients well but fail to explain an unfamiliar product. Specify the audience cost of each choice.

## Verify

Check whether changing only the company name makes the concept equally fit any business. Inspect the complete reading path, mobile order, actual assets and inquiry route. Keep rejected slices and selection reasons. Clean alignment and functional completion do not overrule a weak creative idea or user rejection.
