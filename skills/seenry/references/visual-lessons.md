# Practice a relationship, not a house style

For an uncertain decision, use one applicable craft module and its small runnable example. `scripts/lesson_packet.py TOPIC` retains legacy topic names and returns relevant local source paths and hashes; the stage packet includes their source bodies once. It supplies no reference screenshots, human preferences or claim of premium finish. Render the mechanism with the project's actual content; use inspected external or user-supplied evidence to judge visual craft.

The examples isolate grouping, type roles, color roles, controls, state continuity or composition. Their plain appearance helps inspect the mechanism. It is not an aesthetic target, starter theme or layout to reuse across products. A technical example can pass its checks while still being visually unsuitable for a brief.

Historical screenshots, rejected outputs, source-specific research and one-off preference records live outside the installable skills in the repository's `evals/archive`. They are available for explicit evaluation maintenance, not automatically retrieved by creation or review packets. Preserve user feedback in the current project's DESIGN.md and supply its relevant evidence explicitly.

For reviewer calibration, use `scripts/reviewer_probe.py prepare` with an explicit local manifest of the current evidence. Keep the private mapping and human labels out of the reviewing context. Compare shuffled-order judgments and whether a proposed repair preserves useful behavior. Agreement alone is not correctness. Once a case informs guidance it is development evidence, not a fresh test.
