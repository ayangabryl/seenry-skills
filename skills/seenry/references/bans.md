# Bans

The habits that make a page read as generated. Never do these, unless the person asks for one by name. Many are measured by `review_board.mjs` (through `canvas.mjs verify` and `check.mjs`); the rest are on you. A style brief's own Never list adds to these, never replaces them.

## Layout

- Boxing every element in a card. Separate with space first, a surface tone second, a line last; one method per level.
- The default stack: hero, logo strip, three feature cards, steps, testimonials, FAQ, closing band. Each section takes the form its content needs.
- Three equal cards in a row, or the middle one scaled up.
- Every block at the same weight. Something leads; the rest supports it.
- The same section header three times (title left, small note right).
- Everything centred because nothing was decided.
- Rewriting a whole screen to fix one part. Patch the part.

## Type and colour

- An eyebrow label over every heading, or uppercase letter-spaced labels.
- A two-tone headline with the second sentence in grey, or an italic accent word.
- Monospace as texture on captions and dates. Mono is for code and aligned figures.
- A purple-to-blue or pink glow blob, or a gradient mesh behind every section.
- Pale tinted panels in several hues; the framework's default blue as the accent.
- A coloured dot in front of text that is not a live status.
- A typed hex or pixel value instead of a token.

## Imagery and icons

- Stock people pointing at laptops, isometric 3D blobs, a handshake, a globe.
- Icons in tinted circles, emoji as UI icons, sparkles for AI.
- A screenshot floating at an angle with a soft grey shadow.
- A small boxed photo alone in an empty band.
- One chart in a box standing in for a whole product.
- A cloud of tiny chips to say "many".

## Content

- Lorem ipsum, "Feature one", or a production note on the page (placeholder, demo data).
- Invented customers, quotes, logos or figures, unless the brief says the company is fictional.
- A button, link or shortcut hint with nothing behind it.
- Initials in a circle standing in for a person.
