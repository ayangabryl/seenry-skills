# Hoy: concentration of emphasis

Source: [gethoy.app](https://gethoy.app/), inspected 2026-09-10 in a desktop browser across opening, product explanation and pricing. This is an authored observation, not a Seenry rating or a claim about user-study outcomes. No assets are redistributed.

**Observed:** A playful wordmark incorporating portraits dominates the opening. Short centered copy sits between it and two actions. The primary action has a bright fill; the secondary is quieter. A product demonstration follows. Explanatory prose and pricing details appear later. Rounded pricing panels contrast gently with the canvas. Pills, a small uppercase price label and separators occur selectively. The inspected primary button was about 232×60 CSS pixels with an icon beside its label; this is an observation at that viewport, not a sizing prescription.

**Inference:** Limited competing emphasis, a memorable brand gesture and early product evidence help the simple structure feel considered. The dark palette alone does not explain the effect. Still images do not verify motion timing or accessibility.

**Transfer:** Concentrate attention; show substance; vary emphasis with purpose. A dense professional tool may need more simultaneous controls and explanatory detail. Copying this opening into every brief would erase the very specificity being studied.
