# Compose color combinations, not just a hue slider

Use when authoring a palette, showing color capability, or repairing arbitrary combinations. A single accent with a tinted background demonstrates a control, not a complete color system. Read alongside [color decisions](color-decisions.md) for measurement and role coverage.

Start with the existing identity, imagery and context. Choose a relationship and its allocation: closely related hues for continuity; contrasting families for a focal moment; neutral working surfaces beside expressive artwork; or a dominant brand field with quieter relief. These are hypotheses, not universal harmony guarantees. Equal lightness or mathematically complementary hues can still compete or look unsuitable.

Compare two or three plausible combinations on identical real content. For each, specify canvas, surface, strong/supporting text, action/foreground, selected state, focus and needed status roles. Show it in an expressive opening, a dense content region and a working control/error state. The point is to see the palette hold together as its proportions change, not to make more swatches.

Keep the brightest or most saturated area intentional. A large background can dominate a smaller action even when both pass contrast. Repeat the relationship where recognition matters; vary its proportion where reading matters. Do not assume a brand accent must color every button, icon, label and card. Keep data/status meanings separate from decoration and retain non-color cues.

OKLCH is a construction tool: adjust lightness, chroma and hue independently; reduce chroma when necessary for the output gamut. A copied token should describe the color actually used. Assess actual rendered pairs, including hover/focus and overlays. WCAG ratios do not measure palette beauty. Do not label a whole interface accessible based on one button pair.

Record a reason tied to the offering and an observed weakness of the rejected alternative. If both are merely different, say so. Preserve the user's neutral shell when showing colorful demo content; a capability example does not authorize a site-wide rebrand.

Palette roles, area and pair measurements are the working rules here; no external skill is required. Research provenance is retained in the distribution notices.
