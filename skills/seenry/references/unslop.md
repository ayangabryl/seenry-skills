# Unslop or redesign an existing screen

"Unslop this", "make it not look AI-made", "redesign this dashboard": the person wants the screen to stop looking generated. What gives a screen away today is the default itself: the skeleton every model produces for this kind of screen, every block at equal weight, generic copy and tidy invented data. Tidying the same skeleton has failed.

A small edit to a real app stays on the [existing project](existing-project.md) route.

## What stays and what changes

| Stays exactly | Changes |
| --- | --- |
| Every feature, action, field, filter and data point | Composition: what leads, what supports, how sections differ |
| Information architecture and navigation | Type, color, surfaces and spacing rhythm |
| Behavior and data model | Copy, labels, how numbers and states are shown |
| The project's stack and file layout | Motion, from the motion tokens |

## The steps

1. **Inventory what exists.** List every feature, action, state and piece of data. All of it must still be there at the end.
2. **Name what gives it away.** Look at the screen for one second as a designer would and write down what reads as generated. Those lines are the "Default" in the brief.
3. **Continue with steps 3 to 7 of `SKILL.md`.** Anchor to the category's own leaders as well as products you admire, and put two or three options for the main screen on the canvas, each holding the whole inventory, for the person to pick.
4. **Convention before invention.** Every chart, control and status takes the form people in this category already know. Novelty goes into what leads and how the screen is composed, never into how a status or a table reads.
5. **Check the inventory again.** Every item from step 1 works. A shortcut hint, badge or label with nothing behind it is wired up or removed.

## Report

What changed and how the screen now differs from the default named in step 2, the inventory confirmed item by item, any affordance wired or removed, and what the owner should decide.
