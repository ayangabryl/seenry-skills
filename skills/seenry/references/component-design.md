# Design the component in its host

Use the [content model](content-model.md) to resolve the visible information, image job, control groups and provisional footprint. Carry those decisions into the [component record](component-record.md), then compare them with rendered content.

Establish what the person handles: an image, track, appointment, list or comparison. Record the containing size, inherited identity and surrounding information. Without a supplied host, use a quiet preview canvas and state the assumed width. An embedded component does not need navigation, a promotional introduction or a page footer; a requested standalone tool can legitimately need a larger frame.

Name the task once. Show metadata that affects a choice and group controls by their effect. Keep preview explanations, provenance and test controls outside the product. Resolve the actual footprint and narrow behavior rather than filling a desktop screenshot.

## Compare before committing

Before product implementation, sketch three provisional structures with identical facts. Change a meaningful relationship: direct manipulation versus explicit choices, immediate comparison versus focused editing, or persistent versus disclosed detail. Three palettes on the same structure are surface alternatives, not interaction concepts.

Render only the decisive slice of each. Retain source, captures and the selection reason. A rejected alternative need not be repaired for another to advance. Carry the selected source through typography and the working surface; reopen the direction if real material or interaction fails. Do not regenerate three complete components at every layer.

If alternatives remain interchangeable, inspect the [component family study](studies/component-family.md) for distinct object/action relationships. Adapt those relationships, not the showcase's entire palette or card template.

## Resolve the finish and state family

Finish actual content, typography and material with the [component finish comparison](component-finish.md). Inspect object, choice, output and action together. A written theme is not evidence of good hierarchy. Enclosure, accent and status decoration must serve this task. Useful novelty can come from how an object responds; a slogan above ordinary controls does not resolve its central idea.

Specify input → acknowledgement → actual result → settled state, including interruption and recovery. Keep the target stable and preserve the latest value. Inspect playback: a CSS duration, a working action and a final screenshot establish different things. Load seenry-motion for substantial transitions.

## Carry the relationships

Supplied fonts and real library icons may appear in wireframes. Before finishing, inventory provisional copy, glyphs, coarse boundaries, crops and missing feedback. Resolve them while retaining selected alignment and information relationships. Passing wireframe review does not approve provisional styling.

Share conventions across related states without turning one successful card into every component's template. The earlier exporter failure added unnecessary branding and attempted browser review too late. That motivates scope and chronology checks, not a rule that every component must be small, white or unbranded.
