# Reference standards

Start with relevant 4–5 star references using `search_curated_references`. Select different brands and mechanisms, not many variants of one template. Query the actual family/section. For a full site inspect progression and endings as well as its opening. Pricing needs comparison, billing units and decision clarity; footer/navigation need orientation and return routes. Hero quality cannot establish these alone.

Record **rating target/scope, updated date, saved reason, coverage, observed detail, our interpretation and transfer limit**. An empty reason means “not supplied.” A website rating does not transfer to its motion or every section. Requery before treating an old study as current. Broaden to unrated evidence when the relevant reviewed set is empty and name that limitation.

Compare concrete qualities: focal hierarchy, legible type relationships, subject-specific assets, purposeful grouping, continuity, complete states and responsive behavior. Compare only what the capture shows. Do not claim conversion improvement, keyboard correctness, easing or mobile usability from a desktop still. A high rating does not waive accessibility checks on our output.

For a disagreement, vary one cause in our prototype: remove an unsupported dot, reduce dominant color area, change a type role or compact a label/icon group. Inspect both using the same content. Retain the observation as a scoped regression case, not a ban on dots, colors, serifs or pills.

## Inspected studies, 2026-09-10

These three imported Hero images were inspected through `get_design_reference` and inline `get_reference_asset`. All had **5-star section reviews** at lookup, with **no written reason, use cases or caveats supplied**. The analysis is ours, not the curator's rationale. Source: Unsection; mobile, audio and interaction behavior unverified. No reference pixels or third-party assets are redistributed here.

This is a small calibration set, not a standard covering every category. Research the current brief; add studies when they change a decision. Distinct styles can meet the same quality criteria.
