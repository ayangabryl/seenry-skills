Questions, answered

Is Penny a budgeting app?

Not quite. There are no budgets to set and no envelopes to fill. Penny shows where your money went and what changed, and leaves the decisions to you.

Which banks and cards work?

Over 12,000 banks, credit unions and card issuers in the US, connected through Plaid. If yours is missing, tell us and we will look into it.

Can Penny move my money?

No. The connection is read-only. Penny never sees your bank password and has no way to send, transfer or withdraw.

What if a payment lands in the wrong category?

Tap it and pick the right one. Penny applies the fix to every payment like it, past and future.

Can I share it with a partner?

Yes, with Household. You each keep your own accounts and get one statement for the month you share.
