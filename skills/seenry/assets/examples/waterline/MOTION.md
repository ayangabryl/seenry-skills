# Motion

Signature moment: the app rises out of the water. It is the page's idea (cash under the waterline) in one movement.

| Moves | Trigger | What moves | Duration | Easing | Explains | Reduced motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The app window | Page load, after the headline is readable | Rises 56px from behind the front wave, opacity 0 to 1 | 900ms | ease-out (0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) | The forecast comes up from under the waterline: the product's idea | In its final place, no movement |
| The W9 tooltip | After the app has risen | Opacity 0 to 1, 6px up | 240ms, 700ms delay | ease-out | Points at the one week under the floor, last, so it is read as the finding | Visible from the start |
| Buttons and nav links | Hover, press | Background colour; scale 0.98 on press | 120 to 180ms | ease-out | The control answers the pointer | Colour only, no scale |

Nothing loops. The headline, the lede and all body text are in place at first paint; only the product arrives.
