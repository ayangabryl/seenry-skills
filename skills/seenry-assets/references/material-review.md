# Review the material in context

Inspect actual material at its rendered size, on ordinary and narrow views. Decide whether it clarifies this subject and task; an image-free design can pass. Do not demand an asset merely because a reference used one.

- **Subject and truth:** the visible product, work or identity matches the factual brief. Distinguish illustrative concepts from actual customer or product evidence. Check supplied provenance; do not infer reuse rights from a screenshot.
- **Crop and hierarchy:** the useful subject survives the real crop, neighboring copy and narrow layout. Inspect detailed views when a full-page thumbnail cannot resolve quality.
- **Typography and icons:** actual font loading, long labels, numerals, glyph coverage and fallback metrics preserve hierarchy. Provider marks are genuine; control icons share suitable optical weight and recognizable meaning. Filled versus outline follows the control's role and context.
- **Loading and fallback:** intrinsic dimensions prevent avoidable movement. Missing or delayed media leaves readable content and usable controls. Posters are distinct from verified moving media.

Record the visible issue, task consequence and smallest useful correction. Missing crop, provenance or playback evidence requires inspection, not a speculative redesign. A static capture cannot establish video, Lottie or shader quality in motion; use the motion contract and actual behavior evidence. Keep material findings separate from overall visual acceptance.
