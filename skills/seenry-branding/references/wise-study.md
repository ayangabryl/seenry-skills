# Wise: recognition across expressive and working surfaces

Studied 2026-09-23. Sources: [Wise Design](https://wise.design/), [direction](https://wise.design/direction), [Wise rebrand announcement](https://wise.com/gb/blog/a-brand-for-everywhere-wise-unveils-bold-new-look), and [Seenry reference](https://seenry.design/design/bbf1181a77472aeb22423e4229b6e755). The MCP still (asset index 1) shows three app states. The public design homepage shows applications spanning a card, transport, a campaign and spatial installations. The old public foundation URLs redirected to 404; authenticated internal documentation was not inspected.

## Observed, declared, inferred

**Observed:** the entry and success screens use dense expressive display type and colorful dimensional artwork on white. The card-management screen places an amount, card and compact controls in a quieter hierarchy. Bright green recurs in the card and actions; dark green anchors prominent lettering. The ordinary body and task labels do not copy the display typography's mass.

**Declared:** Wise's 2023 announcement describes its green identity, supporting colors, globally informed imagery and tapestries, and a revised voice. Treat historical product counts in the announcement as dated statements, not current specifications.

**Inferred transfer:** identity can stay recognizable while visual intensity changes with the user's task. Keep a small number of recognizable relationships across a campaign and a dense working screen, rather than treating every surface as an advertisement. Contrast, typography, imagery and language work together; green by itself is not the system.

## Apply the study

For a new identity, build one expressive and one utilitarian application using the project's own content and assets. Compare which relationships persist and which recede. Keep important amounts, controls and error recovery legible. For a research presentation, show the actual cited source and explain those differences; do not relabel a new fictional identity as a Wise reconstruction.

Do not inherit Wise's logo, proprietary type, claims or artwork for another brand. Screenshot-sampled colors are approximate, not official tokens. No timing or interaction fidelity is claimed from this static study. A broad supporting palette needs actual pairing/area tests in the new project; use Seenry's color-combinations guide when available.
