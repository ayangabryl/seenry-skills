One Saturday in June, in the order it happened.

Eleven hours of a real wedding day. I never ask anyone to stop what they are doing, so this is how it looked while it was happening.

7:40a.m.
    1. 7:40 a.m.

      Her mother does the buttons. Neither of them says anything for a while.

    2. 9:55 a.m.

      His father fixes the tie. It was straight the first time.

    3. 1:30 p.m.

      The vows, under an olive tree, in front of forty people who have stopped breathing.

    4. 2:10 p.m.

      Forty minutes later, petals on the chapel steps. Nobody planned the laughing.

    5. 4:45 p.m.

      Twenty minutes alone in the long grass, shoes in hand. This is all the portrait time I ask for.

    6. 7:30 p.m.

      Grandmother, mid-toast, laughing too hard to finish the sentence.

    7. 11:52 p.m.

      The last song. The band is packing up and they are still dancing.

    8. That was one day. Yours is next, if the date is free.

    Questions couples ask

    Can we see a full gallery before we book?

    Yes. Ask for any of the weddings above and I will send the whole gallery, not a highlight reel, with the couple's permission.

    Do you shoot with a shot list?

    No. You give me three groups of people you must not miss and the order of the day. The rest I find as it happens.

    What happens if it rains?

    I carry covers for every camera and I have photographed plenty of weddings in the rain. Wet stone and wool coats make excellent pictures.

    What if we need to move our date?

    Your retainer moves with you to any open date within 18 months, no fee. If I cannot make the new date, it is refunded in full.
