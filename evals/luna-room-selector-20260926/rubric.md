# Fresh Luna design trial rubric

Brief and facts are frozen in `prompt.md`. Keep the same model, effort, tool access and time allowance for comparisons. Review rendered desktop and 320px mobile states without reading author rationale first.

Score each 0–4 with an observed reason:

1. **Task fit:** Can someone compare rooms, choose a slot, see the total and revise the choice without losing context?
2. **Visual direction:** Is the composition specific to an independent audio studio, with a coherent hierarchy rather than generic decoration?
3. **Content integrity:** Are capacity, price, slots and simulated-request language exact and unambiguous?
4. **Interaction:** Do pointer and keyboard paths work, and do rapid changes settle on the latest state?
5. **Responsive finish:** Are 320px, 768px and desktop layouts legible, without clipping or horizontal overflow?
6. **Motion and accessibility:** Do transitions clarify state, remain interruptible and respect reduced motion? Are semantics and focus usable?

Evidence: retain full screenshots, browser interaction notes, runtime errors and any failed checks. A single case can diagnose a defect but cannot prove world-best or broad superiority. Do not convert these criteria to a public claim until independent blinded comparisons and fresh tasks support it.
