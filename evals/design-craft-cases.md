# Reference craft cases for output review

These are observed Seenry MCP references, not templates or bundled media. Their images and signed URLs stay in the library. Reopen the exact source and inspect pixels before using a case; capture dates and coverage can change. A rating is curator input, not a quality metric or conversion result. Each case asks whether a new output solves its own brief with comparable care, not whether it copies the source.

## Website opening: Sqirl

- Source: [Sqirl home capture](https://seenry.design/page/46133854179444cfb45a4037210b34a7), desktop Hero, captured 2026-09-09. The MCP warned that an entrance modal or cookie banner may be present; the image also shows blue selection outlines around menu/cart lettering. Treat those outlines as uncertain capture material, not a design feature. Hidden content or behavior cannot be inferred.
- Observed: an oversized, irregular black word treatment interacts with a plate photograph and line drawing; a small direct heading and table action remain separate. The typography is the main visual object, tied to a restaurant subject rather than a conventional eyebrow/display-serif/subtitle stack.
- Review question: does the output's type have a task or identity role beyond selecting a fashionable face? Is the useful action findable while the subject material remains dominant? Do not transplant the lettering or dish image.

## Service opening: Voy

- Source: [Voy Weight Hero Design](https://seenry.design/design/78126c3637e6fd2b9a3e5c769268df31), imported static section from Unsection. One desktop image was inspected; mobile, motion and live claim behavior are unknown.
- Observed: a clear service promise and action are paired with several coordinated scenes of people, a tangible object and an interface. The material gives more than one view of what the service entails. The written medical and trust claims are source content, not transferable evidence for another product.
- Review question: does the new page's subject material substantiate its own promise at delivery size? Are type and copy specific to its audience, or merely arranged in a similar grid? A single relevant stock photo can still be materially weaker than a coherent produced series.

## App entry: onX Hunt

- Source: [onX Hunt app reference](https://seenry.design/design/006aa64ddf6a77d0a85c0728385eae6e), asset index 2, “Authentication Entry,” from a public app screen collection. The image is one captured state; actual authentication, navigation, contrast under changing map imagery and screen-reader behavior were not observed.
- Observed: a geographic image establishes product context; a dark overlay keeps the center mark and tagline readable; large familiar provider actions and a distinct orange email action organize the bottom decision area. The result uses app-specific subject matter rather than decorative landscape filler.
- Review question: does an app concept preserve its platform's control hierarchy and safe areas while making the available path obvious? Does the background serve the task? Provider marks and source map imagery are not reusable without rights and real integrations.

## Apply the cases

Give the builder the brief and the relevant source image or live MCP result, not these conclusions. After it builds, show independent reviewers anonymous, equally sized output/source captures and the brief. Ask for subject fit, type direction, material quality, action clarity and narrow-state behavior separately. Record exactly which media and interactions were inspected. A repaired output stays labeled as a repair; it does not retroactively turn the original run into a pass.
