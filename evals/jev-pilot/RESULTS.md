## Conclusion and pending work

The optional API adapter works and the decisions are sensible on these three cases. There is no demonstrated quality improvement over the main agent baseline. Full-page baseline/assisted renders, narrow-screen comparison, interaction coverage and blinded human preference remain pending. No new website has been generated by Jev, and no aesthetic parity claim is warranted.
