50/60 exact matches to predefined development labels; 19/20 choices stable under option reversal. These are routing results, not visual-quality scores.

One option reversal incorrectly retained a false “Sent successfully” message. Other mismatches exposed overlapping action definitions. Later clarified probes helped diagnose them; they do not erase the original failures.

No blind human preference or professional-reference parity has been established. Jev is text-only. The primary agent authored and inspected the rendered pages. This is 100 model requests, not 100 complete website generations.
