## Disagreements matter

The false-success case changed from repair to retain after option reordering. It described a local fictional demo claiming Sent successfully even though nothing was sent. This is a material semantic error; demo status must not excuse a false status message.

All 3 invented-metric cases requested observation instead of the expected repair. Three static-preview cases disagreed with a subjective compare label; its repair/observe boundary was ambiguous. Three missing-task cases chose observe rather than none; those supplied options overlapped. The static-preview and missing-task mismatches expose six ambiguous labels. The three invented-metric routes remain unresolved disagreements about repairing a known unsupported claim.

Post-hoc probes 096–098 narrowed the definitions: false-success and invented-metric selected repair; the labeled preview with no observed failure selected retain. This is diagnostic evidence, not a retroactive pass or proof of a general fix.

## Skill changes and limits

Added an empirical failure study to the optional Jev loop guide. Recommendations: apply known truthfulness/function requirements before semantic preference selection; make observe/compare/none meanings distinct; treat order sensitivity as uncertainty; do not combine independent component winners without a coherent page-level review. Budgeted batch runner explicitly supports this authorized experiment; default installed pilot remains 12 calls.21 focused tests passed, including budget exhaustion and 100-call reservation bounds.

No model weights trained. No independent blind human preference. No matched professional-reference parity evaluation. No aesthetic default promoted, no commit or push. The skill remains usable without Jev.
