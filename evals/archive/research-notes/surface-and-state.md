# Surface restraint and state-specific feedback

Use when a functional component still looks heavy or its interactions feel uniformly animated. This is a scoped study, not a list of colors or effects that are universally current.

## Inspected references, 2026-09-20

- [Mobbin pricing](https://seenry.design/#/page/ed7391db1f844735b1ef2131e3e1cb65), captured September 9: white canvas, black primary type/action, quieter gray support, mostly neutral plan surfaces and a small blue savings emphasis. Thin boundaries and dividers remain. The relation to study is emphasis across occupied areas, not “remove every border.” Its large sales headline is not a compact control type scale. Exact font family and CSS values were not established from pixels.
- [Make Reign](https://seenry.design/#/page/e78233fe2118475a8caa2e00688f8c5c), captured September 9: project imagery carries much of the visual identity; large white typography overlays a darkened collection, with a small vivid scroll control. This expressive agency composition is not an appointment-widget template. The library's recording is partial; no claim of full-page choreography inspection.
- [Play layer selection](https://seenry.design/#/design/35365037cbe72f5f06fe3b3322071148): inspected playback samples show selection attached to a changing layer hierarchy, rather than every object acquiring the same decoration. Exact spring parameters and interruption behavior were not measured. Transfer the association between state and its visual object, not the reference's colored layer palette.

The first two sites have five-star editorial ratings with no saved written reason. Ratings do not establish why they work, accessibility, trend prevalence, or suitability. Three references cannot establish “the 2026 style.”

## Apply to an existing component

Inventory simultaneous layers: canvas, outer border, cast shadow, inner panel, colored tile, control boundary, selected fill. Name what each separates or emphasizes. A permanent inline object may need less elevation than a floating menu. Render the same component with fewer redundant layers; preserve necessary focus, selection and boundaries. Judge the visible result rather than count effects removed.

Choose palette roles before accents. Compare neutral and colored treatments on identical geometry and content. A hue repeated across every number, button, tile and muted surface can imply an identity never supplied by the product. Neutrals also need sufficient text contrast and clear state differences. Brand expression, visualization or meaningful categories can justify much more color.

Render actual date/time, long clinician names and action labels. Check weight relationships, numeral spacing and fallback appearance at native scale. Relax compressed display tracking in compact numerals when it harms legibility. Do not transplant marketing headline sizes or infer an approved font family from a reference.

## Map each transition to its owner

| Event | What changes | What remains stable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Choose another time slot | Native checked state, selected treatment, summary, action availability | Focused input, surrounding labels, committed booking |
| Switch date | Selected date and available choices | Date selector geometry and keyboard focus |
| Enter review | Task-specific content, measured region height | Appointment identity and explicit current/proposed values |
| Cancel or close | Expanded region collapses; focus returns | Committed booking; hidden region becomes inert |
| Confirm | Actual committed values update, then optional local value feedback | Truthful success, identifiable appointment, recovery action |

Do not rebuild and fade the entire panel just to acknowledge a radio change. Replacing focused nodes unnecessarily creates focus and continuity problems. Reserve interpolation for the changing region; cancel previous visual work on new input. The application owns truth immediately, even when presentation animates. Live reduced motion must settle values and keep the same focus path. Test repeated input, resizing and reversal, not only screenshots of endpoints.

A local host-authored material study applies these hypotheses to the rejected appointment widget. It is development evidence pending user acceptance and transfer, not proof that a model or Seenry independently reproduces the reference quality.
