# Bridge Signal first-pass trial

This isolated consumer trial used the shortened Seenry entry point and revised typography skill on a fictional footbridge forecast brief. The builder read the source files recorded in `ROUTE.md`, inspected one Seenry weather reference at pixels, rendered two different opening structures, and chose the crossing-window direction. The local prototype uses illustrative data only.

The four study captures and two first complete-page captures are preserved. The study screenshots omit a 15 px browser scrollbar, so their bitmap widths are 1425 and 375 even though the CSS viewports were 1440 and 390. The complete-page captures are 1440 and 390 px wide. Source files are the first complete version; they were not repaired after capture.

An external direction review returned **Revise**: the phone's “Next predicted restriction” label joined words, and the study's rail durations were disproportionate. The complete page fixed the rail widths before first capture, but still joined the label on phone. A fresh full-page review returned **Revise** for that visible copy defect. The rendered first pass avoided the rejected editorial opening and exposed status, restriction time, advice, and forecast action together. That is evidence for this brief, not consistency across briefs.

The builder's nested review gate could not initialize inside its sandbox. External reviews used the preserved images and returned the reports included here. Static images do not verify the sample switch, keyboard behavior, reduced motion, or screen readers; those were not exercised in this trial. `manifest.json` records hashes and dimensions of the preserved files.

A separate parent browser audit after the first captures found no document overflow at 1440, 390 or 320 CSS px. Clicking the 17:30 sample changed the headline to “RESTRICTED” and its advice; focusing the 18:30 sample and pressing Enter changed it to “BELOW 35” with an actual-status caveat; the forecast action reached `#full-forecast`. The same audit confirmed the joined words at 390 and 320 px. It did not test screen readers or motion timing.
