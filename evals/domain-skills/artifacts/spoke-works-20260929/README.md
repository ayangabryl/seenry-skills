# Spoke Works first-pass website trial

A fresh independent builder used the published Seenry skill at commit `3bf3941` for a fictional local bicycle repair shop. The fixed brief supplied three services and prices, four demo slots, hours and a service area. The builder inspected two Seenry MCP booking captures, rejected their map and full-calendar structures for this brief, and used no third-party media or copy. `DESIGN.md` lists the exact skill files and reference IDs read.

Two wide and 390px direction studies changed the phone reading order and treatment. The builder chose the service-first study, then preserved its first complete source and 1440/390/320px full-page renders before external critique. Browser checks passed service and slot selection, price propagation, review/edit, truthful demo confirmation, reset, keyboard Enter/Escape, and no horizontal overflow or page errors at all three widths. These are Chromium checks, not screen-reader or physical touch evidence.

Independent review exposed a gap between functional completion and design direction. The direction study gate returned **Revise**: neither study made its service and time choices visibly selectable, and the alternate study put step 02 before step 01 on phone. The first complete page supplied working controls, but changed the chosen study into a tracked location label, oversized headline with one italic serif word, and small art captions. A fresh first-screen typography reviewer returned **Revise** for that editorial signature and for delaying the first repair and price below the 390/320px opening viewport. The whole-screen first-screen reviewer and the separate full-page reviewer returned **Keep**. Their limits and strengths are preserved in the review summaries; the specialist finding remains blocking. No review-led repair was made to this first-pass source.

The Seenry entrypoint now defines a type-led opening by its visible headline treatment and asks task-led phone studies to show a first real item or choice in the opening viewport. That wording was added **after** this trial. Its preventive effect remains untested. `manifest.json` records source and screenshot hashes and image dimensions. The source references are research evidence, not a copied layout.
